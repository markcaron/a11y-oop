# Scoped accessibility work

Input: task/need ID; requested task and review/fix intent; source, diff, design or running target; routes/states/setup; lifecycle stage; requested role actions; approved standard profile and local policies; library/runtime versions; available methods and capabilities.

Select applicable records from catalog.json. Keep one check ID across roles, providers and lifecycle handoffs. Load only relevant detailed patterns/providers. The catalog is a draft synthesis, not an exhaustive WCAG test suite. Its related criteria require their full applicability conditions and exceptions.

For each finding/result record: schema/check/provider version, task/check ID, target/state, expected versus actual, method/environment, evidence, impact, confidence, owner and verification/retest history. Type: normative violation (substantiated), pattern deviation, inclusive-design observation, product-policy issue or coverage gap. No evidence is not a pass.

Status: Not assessed, Pass, Fail, Not applicable (explain why), Not tested (applicable method unavailable). A checklist completion means scoped work was recorded; it does not prove whole-product conformance.

Keep impact separate from certainty and release priority. Preserve actual research observations, design intent, source analysis, automated outputs, browser observations and human AT evidence as distinct origins. Do not substitute one for another.

Changes and external actions follow the user's requested scope. A review task does not itself authorize unrelated fixes, dependency installation, issue filing or publishing.
