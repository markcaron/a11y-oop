# Plan inclusive research
## Work

Use RES01–02 and relevant task-specific checks. Plan inclusive recruitment, accessible invitations/materials/consent, participant-selected settings and equipment, accommodations and research-tool access. Collect only necessary personal information.

Define meaningful tasks and distinguish hypotheses, reference user stories, simulated walkthroughs and observed participant behavior. Do not generalize one person’s technology or strategy to all disabled people.

Record observed barrier, task/state, access strategy, impact on the task and follow-up owner. Preserve appropriate participant privacy. Keep research evidence separate from expert conformance findings and scanner results; identify useful technical follow-ups.

## Handoff

Deliver a participation plan or appropriately de-identified task findings, according to the request. Carry task IDs into content/design/engineering work. Do not claim a research session proves WCAG conformance.

## Shared context

Read [the contract](../shared/contract.md) for scoped inputs, result states and evidence requirements. Select only applicable rule IDs from [the shared catalog](../shared/catalog.json), using role, task, lifecycle stage and actual target. Related WCAG IDs require applicability/exception review; policy and APG guidance are not automatically WCAG failures.

Use [provider selection](../shared/providers.md) when a design-system component is involved. Framework and provider are independent; support mixed React/Web Component pages. Prefer installed documentation and versioned sources. Missing capability or evidence is Not tested, never a pass.
